Here is the brief description of Soul Script:
Soul Script is a web blog application designed to be a safe and open space where people can share their thoughts, emotions, and life experiences. It’s not just a regular blogging platform—it’s a place where people can express their deepest feelings without judgment, connect with like-minded individuals, and find support from a community that truly understands them.

Why is Soul Script Needed?
In today’s fast-paced world, many people go through stress, anxiety, and loneliness but don’t have anyone to talk to. Social media is full of fake perfection, and not everyone feels comfortable sharing their struggles openly. Soul Script provides a platform where users can write about their emotions, personal experiences, or even daily thoughts. This way, people don’t have to keep their feelings bottled up inside.
key features and benefits :
Emotional Release: Writing is a great way to relieve stress and clear the mind.
Community Support: Users can read, comment, and engage with others, making them feel less alone.
Encouragement & Motivation: Seeing others share their stories of struggle and success can inspire people to stay strong.
Mental Health Awareness: It creates a space where mental health is openly discussed, breaking the stigma.
Non-Judgmental Space: Unlike social media, where people often fear judgment, Soul Script is all about honesty and support.

Installation
 1.Clone the repository:
 
  git clone: https://github.com/madhaverma/web-blog-application
2. Navigate to the project directory:

  cd blog-application
3.Install dependencies:

  npm install
4.Start the application:

  node index.js
5.Open your browser and visit:

  http://localhost:3000
